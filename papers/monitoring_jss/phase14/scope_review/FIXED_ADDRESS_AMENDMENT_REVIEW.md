# Prospective fixed-address workload amendment

26 October 2026. **Recommend adoption now, before new detector-input acquisition, scoring or label joining.** The amendment can be operative while individual address nominations remain pending. It does not admit Li.Fi or any other case. Existing routing investigations, exposures, failures and results remain unchanged.

The article evaluates monitoring decisions under observation and notification constraints. A complete historical protocol population is a stronger target than the existing event-shape methods need. Their inputs are declared addresses, source-validated transaction/log identities, execution status and raw event layouts. They do not consume facet, owner, selector or initializer histories. The narrower estimand is therefore scientifically usable if its selection and missingness rules are fixed prospectively and its claims remain correspondingly narrow.

## Exact population and observation boundary

For a case-chain, freeze a finite address set A from primary historical deployment or component documentation. Define the selected transaction set S as transactions in the unchanged window containing at least one source-observed log whose emitter belongs to A. Obtain all emitted logs and actual execution status for each selected transaction before any arm decides. The count baseline uses the matching core-emitter log subset. Semantic external outflow requires a Transfer-shaped event with sender in A and a nonzero recipient outside A. A spender, operator or approved router is not thereby the Transfer sender.

Expanding selected transactions to all their emitters does not recover transactions outside S. A call to a core address without a core-emitted log may be absent. User-to-external spending through a router can have no core external-out amount. Neither absence permits adding users, victims, token emitters or counterparties to A. Implementation and facet addresses are not automatically emitters; only a separately documented qualifying direct entrypoint or custody role can nominate an address.

The estimand is a retrospective workload and known-incident coverage study for documented fixed addresses. It is not all protocol calls, complete assets under approval, actual balance changes, economic loss or continuous protocol membership. Address nomination takes place now, not at historical deployment time. Past-only feature computation does not make the retrospectively chosen population incident-blind or demonstrate live deployment performance.

## Uniform nomination rule for all 13 rows

Apply one ordered source hierarchy to every original frame row: (1) a historical official deployment manifest identifying the relevant component/version and explicit user-entrypoint or core-custody roles; (2) primary protocol documentation identifying a unique canonical entrypoint, or a finite explicitly coequal set; (3) a primary incident/investigator description of the named protocol component, corroborated by a historical deployment/identity record. Modern addresses without historical relevance and addresses selected from execution transfers, victim lists or detector output are inadmissible nomination evidence.

For a qualifying manifest, enumerate all entries satisfying the declared role predicate within that named artifact. This is completeness within the artifact, not a claim to enumerate all protocol-owned contracts. For canonical-entrypoint documentation, include the complete explicitly documented canonical set. Do not choose a convenient member of an ambiguous set. Material ambiguity leaves nomination pending. Record the chosen artifact/version, historical relevance, source tier, role evidence, every included/excluded entry, exposure and reviewer disposition before payloads. A usable historical primary manifest or canonical entrypoint is sufficient; exhaustive source recovery and every possible writer are not additional nomination conditions.

The third route is expressly incident-informed. An independent deployment check can corroborate identity but cannot make the earlier nomination independent of the incident. Retained Li.Fi and Revest targets require fresh application of the same rule and exposure declaration; they are not admitted automatically. Describe any independence only relative to the frozen detector methods and recorded design history, not global blindness or an incident-independent address population. Ambiguous historical versions must be resolved by metadata evidence, not by observing which version emits useful signals.

## What remains required

The new design needs chain/address and documented core-role identity; the historical deployment/existence bounds actually known; the exact fixed set; continuous source enumeration at a declared evidence tier; correct time/order/status and full selected-transaction bundles; immutable selection/source/observation manifests; and frozen executable method/configuration and label-join procedures. A provider index is not a receipt-root proof. Source unavailability and partial pages cannot be recoded as empty data.

Exact source/runtime compilation and full routing/owner/writer histories remain required only for claims that use those semantics or assert continuous membership. They are not universal prerequisites for the generic address/event-shape workload. Do not claim uninterrupted deployment or a predeployment interval without supporting evidence. Preserve the full 30-day window: a completely enumerated empty day is valid, while an unavailable day is not. Later deployment or cold start must not be hidden by shortening the window.

The method rules remain the Phase 8 contracts: transaction-final count control, per-token positive-outflow ranks, fixed thresholds and warmup, grouping before admission, and the same policy/budget grid. A new multi-case controller may remove Euler-specific input plumbing; it must preserve method semantics and bind its source before affected inputs. The Phase 8 driver resets feature/grouping/admission state per UTC day. Carrying feature state across days is a separate design change, not an unchanged rerun. Freeze the complete window initialization rule explicitly; do not alter it after observing cold start.

## Coverage and outcome denominators

Supersede any use of “observable mechanism” as a favorable-signal eligibility filter for this new estimand. Known realized incidents with no core log, no positive core outflow, cold start or no notification remain in the applicable outer reference denominator. Source gaps remain UNAVAILABLE, and uncertain labels remain UNKNOWN. A known execution with independently verified complete evidence but no selected core-emitter transaction is a coverage miss. Do not insert that execution into detector inputs merely because its label is known.

After decisions close, report the funnel: reference incidents/executions → selected transactions observed → feature-eligible → raw triggers → group candidates → emitted notifications. Keep incidents and multiple execution transactions distinct. Report both all-reference coverage and any explicitly conditional rates; never substitute the observable subset as the only recall denominator. Within a complete verified reference inventory, end-to-end known-execution notification coverage uses all reference executions, including unobservable and cold-start ones. Without a complete positive inventory, no notification on the known hashes establishes known-execution noncoverage, not a proven complete-incident miss. Unknown alerts are not false positives. Missing acquisition is not a measured detector miss.

## Adoption and later measurement boundary

This amendment prospectively supersedes the old dynamic-population continuity gates only for the new fixed-address estimand. It does not retrospectively satisfy them or edit their original protocols. Preserve the 13-row frame, existing development/non-realized dispositions, fixed date/name/row order, mechanism rules, 30-day window rule and prohibition on outcome-based exclusions. Complete a disposition for every row under the same nomination/source rules before freezing the evaluated cohort. Pending rows cannot be skipped because another finishes first. Preserve the minimum of two verified families for that particular cross-family claim; failure to meet it narrows the claim rather than inventing diversity.

Metadata and separately bounded administrative-source capability probes may proceed while nominations are pending. Detector inputs, full workload measurement and label joining require a separate exact evaluation/acquisition freeze covering selected rows, address sets, source limits, missingness, initialization, methods, denominators and label procedures. Case admission remains zero at amendment adoption.

The coordinator should create a hash-addressed acceptance receipt binding `fixed_address_amendment.json`, `frame_status.json`, the executable decision boundary, this review and the independent challenge. The receipt states that the amendment is adopted before affected measurements, that all case nominations remain as recorded, and that the original dynamic-routing question remains unresolved. This records a scientific design boundary under existing user authorization; it is not a request for another user permission.
