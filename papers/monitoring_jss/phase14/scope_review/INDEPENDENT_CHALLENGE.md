# Independent challenge: fixed-address observation scope

26 October 2026. Offline methodological review for Article 2. This document recommends conditions for a possible amendment; it does not adopt one, authorize acquisition or admit a case. No new data, detector results or labels were inspected for this review.

**Recommendation: a prospective, uniform fixed-address, event-observed workload estimand is defensible, but it changes the research target rather than satisfying the unresolved complete-routing gate.** The acquisition failures and earlier routing findings must remain visible. The amendment should answer a useful operational question even if every new incident receives zero notifications.

The original operational objectives concern actual notification load, budget adherence, known-incident coverage, timing and resource costs. Those measurements do not universally require every facet or ownership epoch when their inputs are an explicitly supplied address set and structurally decoded events. Results would concern that fixed stream at the declared source-evidence tier. They would not establish complete protocol activity, complete asset flow, stable routing, prevention or general detection superiority. Changing scope alone provides neither algorithmic novelty nor publication readiness.

## Conditions before affected measurement acquisition

1. **Specify the observable unit.** Freeze address set `A`, transaction-selection rule, feature definition and denominators. All logs of transactions selected by a log emitted from `A` are not all calls to `A` or all protocol activity. A spender is not `Transfer.from`; a delegatecall destination is not automatically another emitter.
2. **Make address nomination uniform.** Prefer a named historical deployment manifest with a fixed role predicate, or a documented canonical entrypoint chosen by a deterministic rule. Verification of ownership and deployment after selecting an attacked address does not make its nomination independent of the incident. A fixed source hierarchy may use incident-informed evidence, but that exposure and the resulting retrospective population definition must be disclosed. Do not describe an unclosed list as all protocol-owned addresses or add victims after inspecting transfers.
3. **Retain the complete candidate frame.** Apply the revised rule to all 13 original rows, preserve catalogue order, family assignments, windows and development exclusions, and finish the same bounded screening procedure across the frame before selection. Availability failures remain recorded. Do not select the first successful download, substitute an easier case outside the frame, or erase the exposure that motivated the amendment.
4. **Close the observation contract.** Freeze source, boundaries, canonical order, execution-status checks, completeness checks at the declared evidence tier, missing-data handling and stopping rules before payloads. Complete administrative history need not be a prerequisite for this estimand; complete observations within its declared source contract still are. Address existence and prehistory rules must be explicit. A proven predeployment interval may be part of a fixed-address workload, but must not become an outcome-driven reason to remove cold-start cases.
5. **Preserve methods and declare initialization.** Keep the fixed detector parameters, grouping and budgets. Resolve the existing Phase8 driver's Euler-specific assumptions and separate-day resets before a continuous-window run. Adapter changes must preserve the algorithm, and all methods must receive the same declared stream. Publish actual emitted load as well as nominal caps.
6. **Freeze independent label handling.** Define incident identity, positive execution inventory, its completeness and notification attribution before the join. Seal and hash all decisions before joining labels. Several execution transactions remain one incident. If the known execution inventory is incomplete, absence of notifications on those hashes supports only noncoverage of the known subset, not a proven complete-incident miss.

## Coverage is not eligibility

| Observed situation | Required disposition |
|---|---|
| Complete accepted observations and independently established execution evidence show no selected core-emitted log | Observation-selection miss; retain the applicable incident in the outer coverage denominator |
| Selected transaction has no qualifying core outflow, lacks warmup, fails the threshold or loses grouping/budget admission | Recorded feature or pipeline outcome, not exclusion |
| Archive, receipt or required context is missing | Unavailable or unresolved coverage; neither an empty history nor a measured miss |
| A row is outside the prospectively defined chain/time scope, explicitly not a realized incident, or already a development case | Documented design exclusion, with its original row and evidence retained |

The former `observable mechanism` condition needs explicit reconciliation so that it cannot become a favorable-signal filter. Report incident counts through successive stages: reference incidents, selected observations, feature eligibility, raw triggers and emitted notifications. A conditional detector result must not hide losses at the observation boundary. Unknown notifications remain unknown without adjudication; they do not supply false-positive rates or precision.
