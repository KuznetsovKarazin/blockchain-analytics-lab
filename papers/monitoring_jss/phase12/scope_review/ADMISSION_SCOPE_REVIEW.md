The accepted phase 9 and phase 10 plan hashes were checked against their acceptance receipts. An unchanged plan's internal `PROPOSED` status is not evidence that its later acceptance never occurred. Conversely, accepting a bounded source-acquisition plan does not freeze an entire scientific evaluation.

**Measurement requirements are narrower than complete routing claims.** The existing count and transfer-observation methods use a supplied address set, canonical log identities and ERC-20-shaped event layouts. They do not consume selector, facet or owner history. Their contracts explicitly disclaim deployed ABI equivalence, token compliance, actual balance changes and complete protocol coverage. For a genuinely prespecified fixed-address workload/allocation estimand, exhaustive writer proof is therefore not a universal scientific prerequisite. Facet changes may change the observed process without changing that address-based measurement definition; delegatecall code targets are not automatically additional emitters.

Continuous dynamic-population or historical-routing claims require the relevant membership/configuration epochs and sufficient evidence for intervening changes. Equal endpoints or a returned administrative-event list alone are insufficient where initializers or other reachable paths can write state. Owner history is relevant when used to establish mutation coverage, not an independent input to the fixed event-shape statistic. A justified complete initial state plus interval history can replace reconstruction from contract creation. Unused source helpers and every conceivable writer are not automatically deployed, reachable or scientifically relevant.

Phase 12 may report discovered administrative events, state checkpoints, inconsistencies, coverage assumptions and unresolved targets. Cap exhaustion or incomplete writer coverage retains **routing unresolved**; it is neither a negative detector result nor permission to expand requests indefinitely. Existing phase 10/11 evidence remains valid within its recorded scope.

A future fixed-address study could use a separately versioned, prospective **all-frame scope amendment before affected measurement acquisition, evaluation or label joining**. It must preserve old routing-unresolved findings and exposure, apply a uniform address-provenance rule, retain frame/order/window/method commitments, freeze complete observation and label procedures, and limit claims to the selected address population. Such an amendment is an explicit design change, not retrospective satisfaction of the current routing gate. It is not adopted here.

Zero observable core flow, cold start, no core-emitted log, weak ranks or no alert must remain measurement/coverage results rather than reasons to exclude a case or add victims to the monitored population. Unknown notifications remain unlabeled without adjudication. Complete observations at a declared source-evidence tier, independent labels and a separately frozen full evaluation remain necessary before any independent-case claim. This disposition directs effort toward the claims the article will make without changing today's scientific commitments.
