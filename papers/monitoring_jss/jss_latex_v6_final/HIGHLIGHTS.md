# Elsevier Highlights — Article 2

- We identify observation, history, feature and notification constraints in Ethereum runtime monitors.
- A 16-core source audit finds 410 of 480 emitter-days below a 20-transaction feasibility gate.
- Three event-count readiness conditions differ sharply: z-score 38.50%, MAD 1.93%, EWMA 99.52%.
- Seven additional replay streams cover 525 transactions and 441 independently checked configurations.
- Rank and event-count rules exhibit conditional, mechanism-dependent complementary notifications.

These results describe selected emitter populations, not a population recall or a prevention guarantee.
