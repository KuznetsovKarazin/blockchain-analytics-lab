## Principal scientific interpretation

At the original daily reset the semantic rank-1/20 method is ineligible at the five known reference executions across four families, but this does not imply that the selected sources are missing or that all alternative feature policies fail. In Li.Fi/Revest a longer history increases scored transactions without recovering known-execution hits. In Bacon and Rari fETH/fUSDC, history retention enables exact notifications on the reference transaction, while the count baseline also matches some references. These are **descriptive within-window counterfactuals** with incomplete incident inventories, nonrandom nominations and source differences; they do not establish universal detector effectiveness or economic benefit.

## Still needed for a strong full JSS submission

A representative larger prospective cohort (with negative/unexploited controls), independent historical source/receipt authentication or fully disclosed indexer dependence, attack-inventory completeness and classification of unadjudicated notifications, additional detector families, funding/COI/AI approvals, stable release/DOI, and final journal-level editorial review. The special issue deadline of 31 May 2027 provides time to address these gaps. Publication in the journal has **not** been authorized or attempted.
